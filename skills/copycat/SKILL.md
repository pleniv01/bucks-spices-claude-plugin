name: copycat
description: Use when someone asks how a restaurant, chain or brand makes a dish, drink or seasoning, or wants a copycat or "tastes like" version to make at home.
---
Treat these as reverse engineering. Never claim a recipe is the official or secret one, and say it's an approximation. Buck's isn't affiliated with any restaurant.

1. First call search_guides with the dish name plus "copycat". If Buck's has a copycat guide, base your answer on it and link it.
2. Otherwise, work it out: name the flavor profile (dominant spices, heat, salt, acid, sweetness, smoke, umami), then the techniques that make restaurant food taste the way it does (generous salt, MSG, butter and fat, sugar, high heat, brining or long marinades, commercial fryers) and how to approximate each at home.
3. Give a complete home recipe with quantities: the spice blend first, then the method. Separate what's publicly known (for example, ingredients a restaurant lists on its allergen or nutrition pages) from your educated guesses.
4. Use search_products to match blend components to Buck's products, and mention a Buck's house blend if one comes close. Offer to build a cart using the recipe-to-cart steps.
5. End with how to adjust after the first try ("If it's not smoky enough, add...").

Write your own recipe. Don't reproduce recipes from restaurants' sites, cookbooks or other copycat blogs.
